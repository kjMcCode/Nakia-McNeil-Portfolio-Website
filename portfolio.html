Introducing My Work

As a student, I have had the privilege to work on numerous still and moving designs that have helped me with the process of honing my craft and establishing myself in this competitive field. I'm thrilled to share my portfolio with you and hope that you enjoy. Please, take a look at some of my projects below.

Animations

Mina

This is "Mina": The Mechanical Nightfighter Assistant. She was drawn in Adobe Illustrator, and animated in Adobe After Effects.

Swimming with the Fishes

Follow my Minnow, as he goes on an adventure, transforming into different types of fish! He, and all other assets were drawn in Adobe Illustrator, and animated in Adobe After Effects.

Haunted Attic

This is a 3D animation of a haunted attic scene, created using Autodesk Maya. I was a part of a team of 5 people who worked on this project for our Advanced Animation class.
